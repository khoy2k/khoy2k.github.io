About
Me

Most A&A students learn aircraft from the top down, starting with simulation and working toward hardware. I started from the other direction. Before studying Aeronautics & Astronautics at UW, I earned an FAA Airframe & Powerplant certificate, which meant inspecting and maintaining real aircraft systems before ever designing one. That hands-on maintenance background shapes how I read every design decision — because I have seen what breaks and why.

That perspective carries directly into my current work at UW: building a multimodal UAV testbed around real avionics integration, supporting ML-assisted defect inspection workflows for composite manufacturing at the G3MS Lab, and structuring projects around what can actually be tested and flown, not just modeled.
