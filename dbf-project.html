Overview

I founded and led an AIAA DBF-style aircraft subteam within the EvCC STEM Club, choosing a learn-by-building approach over immediately targeting competition requirements.

The team grew to 7 members across Aeronautics and Mechanical tracks. Design work progressed through aerodynamic sizing, airfoil selection, wing structural layout, and a SolidWorks CAD model. A small-scale model was built and flight-tested, revealing CG and structural issues. The project was discontinued before full-scale build or flight.

This page is structured as a reflection and reconstruction. The first half documents what the team actually did. The second half revisits those decisions using current Aeronautics and Astronautics coursework to identify what should have been done differently.
