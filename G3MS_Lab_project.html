Overview

This page documents my ongoing undergraduate research at the G3MS Lab, University of Washington, under Professor Avin Vijay. I joined in March 2026 as part of a planned transition to support the lab's automated fiber placement and additive manufacturing projects as senior researchers graduate. This page will be updated as the research develops.

Current Work

My current focus is on building hands-on understanding of the AFP manufacturing workflow before contributing to the software and ML-based defect identification side of the project. Contributions to date include participating in composite prepreg layup, documenting coupon-level defects using microscope imaging, organizing photo documentation of AFP coupons, and supporting Filabot filament extrusion trials for the additive manufacturing project.

Next Step

As the project develops, I plan to contribute to the ML-assisted defect identification pipeline, connecting the physical defect documentation work to image-based classification methods. Updates will be added to this page as new work is completed.
